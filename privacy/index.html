Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CBHR (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our websites and applications under cbhrcom.com, including Reddit/Devvit apps and related automation.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you provide: contact details or form submissions you send us.
  • Usage data: basic technical logs needed to operate and debug services.
  • Public platform content: publicly available posts/comments you request analysis for.

2. How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve our services
  • To generate insights and deliver requested analysis results
  • To communicate about support or service updates
  • To protect against abuse and comply with legal obligations

3. How we share information

We may share information with service providers that help us run the product when necessary to fulfill a request you initiate. We do not sell personal information.

If you connect an external webhook or automation endpoint, analysis results you generate may be sent to that endpoint under your control.

4. Data retention

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes described above, unless a longer period is required by law.

5. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Your choices

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law.

7. Children’s privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. International processing

Information may be processed in Canada, the United States, or other locations where we or our providers operate.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the services after updates means you accept the revised policy.
